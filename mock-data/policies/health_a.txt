COVERAGE
In-patient hospitalisation - covered up to sum insured
ICU charges - covered up to Rs. 10,000 per day
Room rent - covered up to Rs. 5,000 per day
Pre-hospitalisation expenses - covered for 30 days before admission
Post-hospitalisation expenses - covered for 60 days after discharge
Day care procedures - covered (listed 540 procedures)
Ambulance charges - covered up to Rs. 2,000 per hospitalisation
Domiciliary hospitalisation - covered subject to doctor certification
Organ donor expenses - covered up to Rs. 1,00,000
Maternity - not covered
Dental treatment - not covered unless caused by accident
Cataract surgery - covered up to Rs. 40,000 per eye after 24 months

WAITING PERIODS
Initial waiting period: 30 days from policy start date (not applicable to accidents)
Pre-existing diseases: 36 months
Specified illnesses (hernia, cataract, joint replacement): 24 months

LIMITS AND CONDITIONS
Room rent limit Rs. 5,000 per day; proportionate deduction applies if a higher category room is chosen
ICU limit Rs. 10,000 per day
Deductible of Rs. 10,000 applies to each reimbursement claim
Co-payment of 10% applies for insured members above 60 years
Cashless facility available only at network hospitals
Claim intimation must be made within 48 hours of emergency admission or 72 hours before planned admission
Reimbursement claims must be submitted within 30 days of discharge

EXCLUSIONS
Cosmetic or plastic surgery unless necessitated by accident
Treatment for alcoholism, drug or substance abuse
Self-inflicted injury or attempted suicide
Experimental or unproven treatment
Treatment outside India
War, nuclear or terrorism related injury
Maternity and childbirth related expenses
Dental treatment not arising from an accident
Weight control programmes and obesity treatment
Infertility and sterility treatment

CLAIM PROCESS
Intimate the claim through the COVE2E app or the insurer helpline
For cashless claims present the health card at a network hospital
For reimbursement claims collect all original documents at discharge
Submit the claim form with the required documents within 30 days of discharge
The insurer may raise a query for additional documents; respond within 15 days
Settlement is made to the registered bank account after approval

REQUIRED DOCUMENTS
Duly filled and signed claim form
Copy of policy schedule
Identity proof of the policyholder
Original itemised hospital bill with payment receipts
Discharge summary from the hospital
Medical certificate from the treating doctor confirming diagnosis and treatment
Prescriptions and diagnostic reports where applicable

NETWORK
7,800 network hospitals across India
